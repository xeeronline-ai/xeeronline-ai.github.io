Privacy Policy

Color Splash · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Overview

Color Splash is a coloring game for children, made by XEER Technology. We built it so that children can play safely. This policy explains what happens with information when the app is used.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, photos, location or any other personal details, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

Drawings and settings

Pictures your child colors, and settings such as music and sound, are saved only on the device. They are never uploaded or sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

New pictures

When the device is online, the app downloads new coloring pictures from GitHub, where we publish them. As with any download, GitHub's servers receive the device's IP address and basic technical details of the request. The app sends no personal information with these downloads. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Ads

The app may show ads through Google AdMob, for example an optional video a child can choose to watch to unlock a picture. All ad requests are marked as directed at children, so Google does not show personalized (interest-based) ads or remarketing ads, and limits ads to content rated suitable for children. To serve and measure ads, Google may receive the device's IP address and technical details such as the device model and operating system. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

The app may offer an optional one-time purchase that unlocks all pictures and removes ads. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store payment details. Purchase buttons are placed behind a parent check so that children cannot buy by accident.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we use only third-party services that are certified for use in apps for children.

Changes to this policy

If we change the app in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please email us at sales@xeertechnology.com.
